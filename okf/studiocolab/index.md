# About
**Growing collaborations for a better future.**

## What

StudioCoLab is a resource commons for project-focused groups to build community, products, and services.

## Why

While capitalism drives our economy, it also fuels competition and inequality. Corporate self-interest prioritizes growth over quality of life. The imbalance feeds a cultural psychosis that ravages ecosystems, destabilizes governance, and fuels epidemics of loneliness and division.

## How

StudioCoLab organizes tools and resources to build shared projects. We host workflows and events to cultivate trust, incubate ideas and execute plans. We promote a worker-owned cooperative business model for market-viable projects. Revenue-generating projects can subscribe to StudioCoLab&#x27;s services, supporting its work.

## When

StudioCoLab is in early active development. Check back soon for what&#x27;s new. Now is a great time to [join a project](/all-projects) to have an impact or to start a new project. [Subscribe](/engage) for occasional updates.
