[StudioCoLab](index.md)[StudioCoLab](index.md)# Mutual Credit Systems

Mutual credit is an alternative exchange mechanism where participants hold accounts in a community ledger.
- Account balances represent a commitment to pay back the community.
- A zero-sum accounting system: all balances sum to 0.
