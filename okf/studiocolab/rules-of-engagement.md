[StudioCoLab](index.md)# Rules of Engagement

To keep the collaborative space productive, safe, and transparent, all members must adhere to the following rules:

- **Show respect:** Always communicate constructively.
- **Document updates:** Note significant details in docs.
- **Privacy First:** Only use screen names. Do not display user emails.

> [!WARNING]
> Violating these rules may result in temporary suspension of access.
